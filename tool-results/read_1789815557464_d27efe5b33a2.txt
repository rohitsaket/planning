   197→   197→   197→======================================================================
   198→   198→   198→
   199→   199→   199→The current deterministic sales calculation is authoritative.
   200→   200→   200→
   201→   201→   201→A stone qualifies for sales demand only when:
   202→   202→   202→
   203→   203→   203→Lot Status DB = Invoice
   204→   204→   204→
   205→   205→   205→AND its Doc Date is inside the inclusive rolling 90-day window.
   206→   206→   206→
   207→   207→   207→The window is:
   208→   208→   208→run date + previous 89 calendar dates.
   209→   209→   209→
   210→   210→   210→For each planning category:
   211→   211→   211→
   212→   212→   212→90-Day Sales Pieces
   213→   213→   213→=
   214→   214→   214→COUNT qualifying Invoice stones.
   215→   215→   215→
   216→   216→   216→Monthly Average
   217→   217→   217→=
   218→   218→   218→90-Day Sales Pieces / 3.
   219→   219→   219→
   220→   220→   220→Unrounded Target Stock
   221→   221→   221→=
   222→   222→   222→Monthly Average × 2.
   223→   223→   223→
   224→   224→   224→Target Stock:
   225→   225→   225→round conventionally to the nearest whole piece.
   226→   226→   226→
   227→   227→   227→Exact .5 rounds UP.
   228→   228→   228→
   229→   229→   229→Examples:
   230→   230→   230→5.33 → 5
   231→   231→   231→5.50 → 6
   232→   232→   232→5.67 → 6

   234→   234→   234→Do NOT use:
   235→   235→   235→- banker's rounding;
   236→   236→   236→- ceiling rounding;
   237→   237→   237→- JavaScript floating-point shortcuts that create boundary errors.
   238→   238→   238→
   239→   239→   239→Use deterministic decimal-safe logic.
   240→   240→   240→
   241→   241→   241→Available Finished Stock:
   242→   242→   242→count only qualifying polished Stock records according to the current confirmed rule.
   243→   243→   243→
   244→   244→   244→Initial Shortage:
   245→   245→   245→MAX(0, Target Stock - Available Finished Stock).
