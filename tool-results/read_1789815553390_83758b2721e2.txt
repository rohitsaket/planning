   196→4. CONFIRMED DEMAND CALCULATION
   197→======================================================================
   198→
   199→The current deterministic sales calculation is authoritative.
   200→
   201→A stone qualifies for sales demand only when:
   202→
   203→Lot Status DB = Invoice
   204→
   205→AND its Doc Date is inside the inclusive rolling 90-day window.
   206→
   207→The window is:
   208→run date + previous 89 calendar dates.
   209→
   210→For each planning category:
   211→
   212→90-Day Sales Pieces
   213→=
   214→COUNT qualifying Invoice stones.
   215→
   216→Monthly Average
   217→=
   218→90-Day Sales Pieces / 3.
   219→
   220→Unrounded Target Stock
   221→=
   222→Monthly Average × 2.
   223→
   224→Target Stock:
   225→round conventionally to the nearest whole piece.
   226→
   227→Exact .5 rounds UP.
   228→
   229→Examples:
   230→5.33 → 5
   231→5.50 → 6
   232→5.67 → 6
   233→
   234→Do NOT use:
   235→- banker's rounding;
   236→- ceiling rounding;
   237→- JavaScript floating-point shortcuts that create boundary errors.
   238→
   239→Use deterministic decimal-safe logic.
   240→
   241→Available Finished Stock:
   242→count only qualifying polished Stock records according to the current confirmed rule.
   243→
   244→Initial Shortage:
   245→MAX(0, Target Stock - Available Finished Stock).
   246→
   247→Keep these fields separately:
   248→- 90-day sales;
   249→- monthly average;
   250→- unrounded target;
   251→- rounded target;
   252→- available stock;
   253→- physical shortage.
   254→
   255→Never hide the intermediate calculation.
