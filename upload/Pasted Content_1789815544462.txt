4. CONFIRMED DEMAND CALCULATION
======================================================================

The current deterministic sales calculation is authoritative.

A stone qualifies for sales demand only when:

Lot Status DB = Invoice

AND its Doc Date is inside the inclusive rolling 90-day window.

The window is:
run date + previous 89 calendar dates.

For each planning category:

90-Day Sales Pieces
=
COUNT qualifying Invoice stones.

Monthly Average
=
90-Day Sales Pieces / 3.

Unrounded Target Stock
=
Monthly Average × 2.

Target Stock:
round conventionally to the nearest whole piece.

Exact .5 rounds UP.

Examples:
5.33 → 5
5.50 → 6
5.67 → 6

Do NOT use:
- banker's rounding;
- ceiling rounding;
- JavaScript floating-point shortcuts that create boundary errors.

Use deterministic decimal-safe logic.

Available Finished Stock:
count only qualifying polished Stock records according to the current confirmed rule.

Initial Shortage:
MAX(0, Target Stock - Available Finished Stock).

Keep these fields separately:
- 90-day sales;
- monthly average;
- unrounded target;
- rounded target;
- available stock;
- physical shortage.

Never hide the intermediate calculation.
